We develop an adaptive video-based planning system that incorporates interaction-time feedback to handle uncertainty. By refining its latent state representation and filtering out failed plans, the framework adapts without explicitly modeling hidden parameters. Tested on a new manipulation benchmark, it significantly improves replanning and robustness in video-driven control.
